How are we to live?

Evidence-based pointers on living well. If you don't care what happens next, or whether your beliefs are true, you'll not find anything useful here.

  • Trust but verify both clocks
  • Smoking kills, stopping helps both clocks
  • Alcohol: no safe dose both clocks
  • Gambling: designed so you lose both clocks
  • Eat food. Mostly plants. Not too much. both clocks
  • Move more, sit less both clocks
  • Sleep like it matters 10-yr clock
  • Slip, slop, slap both clocks
  • Change the rules, not just yourself both clocks

In a hurry? The short version boils it all down to five things for each age — each one linked back to its evidence.

How to read each page

  • Pick your age. Each page has three tabs — 8–11, 11–16 and 16+. The facts are the same; the explanation changes.
  • Which clock it's on. The ten-year clock means the next ten years of your life — the things that could affect you soon. The seventy-year clock means your whole life ahead, roughly the next seventy years — things that build up slowly and mostly show up much later. Some pages are on both.
  • How sure we are. From near-certain down to contested.
  • The evidence, and its weak spot. Each page ends with the studies behind it, described in plain words, and the honest weakness in the evidence. Stuck on a word? See Words we use.
